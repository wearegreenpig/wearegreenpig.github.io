Reading Yeom Sang-seop

About the work

Through Yeom Sang-seop's early short stories, a self-portrait of modern Koreans. Just as Yeom saw Japanese imperialism as something to be overcome, the piece views capitalism as something to be overcome and reflects the age we live in against the Japanese occupation period. The stories Dark Night and Rice were read as theatrical readings of the fiction itself, while Autumn Night (Jeon Sunghyun) and Mamma (Ju Eungil) were read as plays rewritten for today, with a discussion featuring commentary and a lecture by Lee Jongho.

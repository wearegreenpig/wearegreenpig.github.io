Snapshot

About the work

A photograph records, in a single image, a moment in which events and emotions are intricately mixed, and we accept it as reality. To a gaze that has lost imagination in a world full of images, the piece adds the gaze of the Little Prince, who sees the world with the eyes of the heart, and talks about the essence of photography. Starting from Cartier-Bresson's "decisive moment," it asks whether documentary photography can be a window onto the world, and whether we can experience the world through photographs.

Midsummer Train Game

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. July. Since 2017, when I started doing theater, every July I have been performing or rehearsing. Can that routine and everyday life become history? Devised by Kang Boreum with Green Pig's performers.

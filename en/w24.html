1984

About the work

Based on George Orwell's novel 1984, it starts from the fact that the future Orwell foresaw was the year 1984. Between a 1984 already lived through and a future yet to come, it gauges our coordinates and seeks the paths of life an individual can take within the whole called the nation or society. A work that showed unbridled imagination in social statement through aesthetic experiment.

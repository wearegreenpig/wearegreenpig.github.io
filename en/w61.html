A Good Place to Live, OO

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. June. In June 2023 Park Hyunji had to move out of the house she had lived in since 1995 because of redevelopment. That same month, police and city hall faced off over road use at the Daegu Queer Culture Festival. A story about private space, public space and their histories.

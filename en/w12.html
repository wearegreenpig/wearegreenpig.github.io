Anyway, Baek Seok

About the work

Tracing the life of the genius poet Baek Seok, it looks for the joints where life becomes art and art becomes life. We will remember not the poems Baek Seok left but the life he left. The first work in Green Pig's "artist series."

Performances

2011.11.9–13 / Theater Lab Hyehwa-dong 1beonji

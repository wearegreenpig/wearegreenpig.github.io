Phantom Neuro

About the work

Individual anxiety arises from social rules, conventions and standards and appears in the body as symptoms. An immersive performance that explores cases of anxiety such as the violence of the gaze, average income, appearance and pressure to succeed through the performers' bodies, and, by way of Michel Foucault's "utopian body," lets the audience sense the gallery's floor and walls, light and noise strangely with their own bodies. Made with texts by the poet Jang Suyang.

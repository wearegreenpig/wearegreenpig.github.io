Contemporaries

About the work

Forty-five scenes for two people. The scenes have no order, and the place where each conversation happens is its title. In different places, different characters talk about different subjects such as dream interpretation, math problems and blind dates. Instead of looking for heroes, a play that tries to think the contemporary as contemporary, an age that is neither anyone's nor anything's.

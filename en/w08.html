Step-memory: Return of the Repressed


2012, Festival/Tokyo

About the work

Taking "war" as its theme, it speaks through soil about the silent pain that has faded beneath the land we live on and about senses that persist, repeat and are forgotten. The third work in Green Pig's series of "relentless reflections on form," it combines acting, music, video and visual art rather than realizing a play script, expressed through the tracing and juxtaposition of severed events, images and oral testimonies. Through testimonies of repressed memory it faces historical events and calls back and mourns those who died wounded, out of unrecorded history.

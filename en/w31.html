The Whistleblowers

About the work

"Will you still come forward as a whistleblower?" A performance made to put this question to the audience. A corporate executive who finds a massive slush-fund ledger, a professor who learns of a colleague's admissions fraud, a Red Cross employee who discovers mismanagement of blood supplies, a high-school student who refuses the school's religious coercion…. Internal whistleblowers who exposed injustice try and fail to fix things inside their organizations, finally go public, and face pressure and attacks from the press, are pushed out of their organizations and sometimes lose their health and families. Within a non-dramatic structure, the piece makes a triple conflict, inner struggle, ostracism inside the organization and distortion by society and the press, erupt in succession.

Performances

2017.9.22–10.15 / Naon Theater

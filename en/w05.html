Grief Not Fully Mourned

About the work

A work made to mock and satirize to the full the blindness and undemocratic nature of the power of May 1980. Though full of absurd and comic elements, the performance does not openly reveal that its target is "the power of May 1980." The protagonists keep producing laughter through ridiculous accidents, and through that laughter the audience meets the May 18 Democratic Uprising from a new angle. Part of Arko Arts Theater's "Praise of Creation."

Performances

2008.8.1–10 / Arko Arts Theater, Small Hall

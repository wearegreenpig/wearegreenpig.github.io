Home Sweet Home: Dorothy

About the work

Where do actresses go when they have children? Why do they disappear? Can pregnancy, childbirth, child-rearing and doing theater coexist? A fairy tale that does not end with "they lived happily ever after," looking into the hearts of people who were swept up one day by a sudden whirlwind and dropped into a world they had never known, and who, having barely left Kansas, choose to go back to Kansas.

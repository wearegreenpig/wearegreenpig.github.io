About the work

A city sunk under water. On one side stands a dam holding back the water; the other is open to the sea. On the last remaining island live the people who rule; on rooftops flooded with water live the ruled. Disasters never stop, so why doesn't the world end? To find the answer, a thought experiment that locks the world inside the theater, causes disasters and watches how people behave in it.

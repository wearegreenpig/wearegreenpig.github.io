The Great Battle of the Sheep Ranch

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. March. Inspired by Sero, the zebra that escaped from a zoo in March 2023 and was caught three and a half hours later, this is the story of sheep who step outside their peaceful ranch. Borrowing the mouths of non-humans, it asks about the right to refuse and resist, for humans and non-humans alike who live domesticated lives.

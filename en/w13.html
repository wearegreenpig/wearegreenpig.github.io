Brain Surgery


Photo: Mr-Hodol

About the work

A renowned doctor swaps the brains of Sangdo, the simple-minded son of a rich family, and Mugil, a poor but brilliant country youth, and chaos follows. Without altering Jin Uchon's 1945 play, the production adds B-movie thriller staging, a chorus of pro-Japanese songs and a news article reporting "the happy ending of the brain surgery," so that the text itself is objectified and looked at. Yoon Hansol calls this approach "self-contradictory comedy."

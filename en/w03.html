Days Like a Painting

About the work

A play built from stories drawn from the genre paintings of the late Joseon painter Hyewon Shin Yun-bok. The death-bound love of the gisaeng Haeeosu and the scholar Suseok who is obsessed with her, the love that abandons everything of Seohui, deserted by her husband, and Huiyun who sets out with her, and the story of the maid Dallae who loves in order to live together, follow and cross one another like the pages of an album. The original title was Where Are You Now.

Performances

2007.5.19–6.3 / Seoul Arts Center, Jayu Theater

Becoming Korean

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. December. Begun in mid-December 2023, before the month had ended, at a point when no one could know what would happen or which history would be told.

Performances

2024.12.6–15 / Art Space Hyehwa - History Dispute Project, December

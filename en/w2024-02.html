Real Theater: The Immortal Lee Kiseok

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. February. Who makes "great figures," and why? Who wrote the biographies we read as children? Based on actor Lee Kiseok's February 2023, the piece traces the process by which a great figure is "made to be born."

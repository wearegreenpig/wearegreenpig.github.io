Who Landed a Missile Punch on Muhammad Ali's Temple?

About the work

A "concertrama" made by Green Pig and the band Yalgaedeul, in which a concert and a drama put on a show on one stage. Presented by the Seoul Foundation for Arts and Culture, co-produced by Namsan Arts Center and Green Pig.

Performances

2010.11.26–12.5 / Namsan Arts Center Drama Center

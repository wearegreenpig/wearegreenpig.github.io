The Way of Story, the Way of Dance – Gong Ok-jin's Byeongsin-chum

About the work

A sequel to The Way of Story, the Way of Song – Demo Version (2014). After pansori, this time it explores Gong Ok-jin, master of byeongsin-chum (the "cripple dance"), and the one-person changmu-geuk. Starting from the idea of whether byeongsin-chum could be learned through a game using a kinetic sensor, it attends to Gong Ok-jin through the Japanese occupation, liberation, the Korean War and her changgeuk troupe years, and after she became known to the public through her Space Sarang performances. Following what the kinetic sensor can and cannot read, it "presents" byeongsin-chum in the now, and Gong's students appear onstage. Part of the 2018 Namsan Arts Center season and an official Korean selection of the 25th BeSeTo Festival.

Performances

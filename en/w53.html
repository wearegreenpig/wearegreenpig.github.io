Ju Eungil One-Act Plays

About the work

A one-act program combining The Mountain Speaks, winner of the 2023 Busan Ilbo Spring Literary Contest for drama, and the short play We Are Harmonious Flowers. The Mountain Speaks is a picaresque in which flawed characters on three mountains in different times move toward what they each want; We Are Harmonious Flowers is about lovers who endlessly repeat parting and reunion.

History Exploration Lab

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. May. People or anything else, a sigh a second ago or an explosion 13.8 billion years ago: everything leaves traces. A joint research project declaring that only the traces of everything are real history, so that selected history cannot pass itself off as reality.

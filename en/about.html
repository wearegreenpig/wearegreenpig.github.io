Subversive Imagination: Green Pig

A performance company made by people with the conviction that ideas change the world and with burning sensibility. We are people who came together to make theater that diagnoses a civilization proceeding without doubt, or without doubting. We are people who came together to make theater that thinks about progress in subject matter and artistic form. Green Pig's work is a manual, or a tool, for resistance or exodus.

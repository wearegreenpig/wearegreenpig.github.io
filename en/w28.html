Behind Cut

About the work

A company that has agreed to perform Oh Jongwoo's A Sculptor and a Detective at a festival of banned plays. During rehearsals the director suddenly disappears, and with only the company members left it becomes unclear whether the show can go on. Part of "Bill of Rights 2016: Censorship Dismissed," which responded to the theater blacklist scandal.

Performances

2016.9.29–10.2 / Yeonwoo Theater - Bill of Rights 2016: Censorship Dismissed

The Way of Story, the Way of Song – Demo Version

About the work

A work interpreting the pansori Jeokbyeokga through the theme of "tradition." It stages the very process of understanding and expressing pansori: finding the meanings of words to learn Jeokbyeokga, learning dialect, and imitating gestures with exaggeration. In the attitude due to tradition, the performers kneel throughout the show, and if a phone rings or vibrates in the audience, the performance stops. It began at the autumn festival of Hyehwa-dong 1beonji's 5th members and was invited to TPAM in Yokohama in 2016.

Performances

2014.3.20–30 / Theater Lab Hyehwa-dong 1beonji
2016.2.10–14 / KAAT, Yokohama - TPAM 2016

Man Is a Wolf to Man

About the work

A woman in her late twenties throws herself onto subway tracks and dies. All that identifies her is a ring and a greeting card; the CCTV footage has vanished, and the high-school girls who witnessed it left before the police arrived. At the same hour, a confrontation over a marriage-fraud case continues at a police station. As seemingly unrelated incidents are woven into one, the play reveals how we come to stand at the end of a tangled skein of guilt regardless of our intentions, as a frog dies from a stone thrown carelessly.

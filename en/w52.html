Extra Chronicles


Photo: Yu Kyungoh

About the work

During the Japanese occupation, an independence fighter occupies the top of a wooden telegraph pole, determined not to go to Manchuria but to stay on this land and remember everything that happens. The occupation passes on to a sergeant occupying an ammunition depot, a believer occupying a confessional, a worker climbing onto a factory roof and an activist up a tree to stop development. Across a hundred years unfold the stories of occupiers, or extras.

Harry

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. November. On the longest night of the year, five creators write out the everyday text called "I": the I who is a perpetrator of discrimination and exclusion. A character analysis of eleven months, written at the height of winter.

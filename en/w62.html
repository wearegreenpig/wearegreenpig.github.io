Hoo-Ha!

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. August. Taking part in the Jamboree requires a fee, a uniform and plenty of gear. At prices that high, some boys start camping on their own just to get a taste of it from afar. The piece sets the August 2023 Saemangeum World Scout Jamboree fiasco against people enjoying summer outside it.

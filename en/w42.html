About the work

About how fake news is made and spreads and the phenomena it causes. Misinformation that spread like an epidemic during COVID-19, rumors that amplify hatred and discrimination, stock-market tip sheets and stories in messenger apps. In a world full of fake news, it asks what we should believe and how we should live. The second work of the Fake News Research Project.

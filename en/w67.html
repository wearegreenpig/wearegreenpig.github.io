Tropical Night in Deep Winter

About the work

A work that began with the thought, after the martial law declaration of December 2024, that the story of its instigators had to be told. Drawing the villains sometimes roughly as demons and sometimes as ridiculous third-rate comedians, it tries to record in theater a history that must not be forgotten.

Yeol, born on the day a comet fell, decides amid his parents' expectations to become a righteous prosecutor. After failing the bar exam eight times he studies nothing but the Constitution for nine years, becomes a "Constitution master," and enters the center of power; passing through the VIP room, the master monk Mangong and Juliet, he is gradually consumed by madness and becomes president. In the end he declares martial law and tries to seize the city. The play is built from the deflated conversations of theater people watching all of this, and the line between reality and fiction collapses.

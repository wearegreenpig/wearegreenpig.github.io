Tree Won't Seek for Shoe Store

About the work

Sets the paradox of civilization's progress and humanity, represented by "shoes," against Yoon Yeongseon's "tree." Over Yoon's poetic, melancholic language, short episodes such as "Monkey's Flower Shoes," "Fetal Chatter" and "Contract" cross at a quick pace. On a mountain of waste piled from garment-factory scraps, it depicts nuclear power, unstoppable desire and capital, and a collapsing civilization through the eyes of "a fetus that does not want to be born." It began from Yoon's seven-page draft and went through devising and Jeon Sunghyun's additions and restructuring; first staged at the 2012 Yoon Yeongseon Theater Festival and revived ten years later. The start of Green Pig's "e (environment) trilogy."

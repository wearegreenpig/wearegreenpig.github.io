Jin and Jun

About the work

A story of triplets in seven scenes: they must meet but are apart, together yet separated. Lovers, North and South, citizens of the world and our history unfold. The sixth production in Seondol Theater's series "Standing at Seondol."

Performances

2008.12.30–2009.2.1 / Seondol Theater, Daehangno

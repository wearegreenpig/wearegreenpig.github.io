Fear

About the work

Based on Anton Chekhov's short story "Terror," written after his journey to Sakhalin, a play that newly recasts the story's narrator "I" as Chekhov himself. Back from Sakhalin, Chekhov visits his friend Silin, who runs a country farm: Silin's wife Marie, whom Chekhov knew before her marriage; the servant Gavrila, once dismissed for his drinking; and Silin, who loves a woman who despises him. Through people confused before incomprehensible lives, it explores human nature and the essence of life.

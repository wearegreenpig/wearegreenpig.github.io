When Chorok Lies, We All Clap

About the work

A work that refuses the coming-of-age narrative of youth and puts forward "the impossibility of growing up." It takes apart the linear, well-behaved story structure familiar from youth theater and turns it into an experimental play.

Performances

2025.8.22–31 / Sogang University Mary Hall, Small Stage - Young Artists Leap support

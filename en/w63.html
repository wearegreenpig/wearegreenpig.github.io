The Nauseator (It)

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. October. We catch ourselves, when there is someone we hate, searching the mass media for clues that make it all right to hate them. Starting from the powerful "aura" that mass media produces, the piece rereads an event of October a year earlier.

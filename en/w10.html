I'm the King of Sex

About the work

A work reconstructed from a series of humanities texts on narcissism, including Freud's "On Narcissism: An Introduction." Starting from concepts such as autoeroticism, the sexual ideal and the ego ideal, it portrays a person who, despite ample sexual knowledge and desire, cannot form relationships with others.

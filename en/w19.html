Desdemona: A Play About a Handkerchief

About the work

While the events of Shakespeare's Othello unfold offstage, a story of the "hidden schemes" of three women, Desdemona, Emilia and Bianca, in a back room of the palace. Paula Vogel turns Shakespeare's women from innocent victims of men into people who act in the face of their fates, overturning the tragedy with sharp satire and comic transformation.

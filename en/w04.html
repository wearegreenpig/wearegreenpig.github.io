Without Vinker I'm Too Lonely

About the work

Before the show the audience receives a cassette tape recording texts about God, and plays it together when the performance begins. Prerecorded lines and the performers' lines sound simultaneously or in turn, and text, movement, live music and song mingle like a carnival. Attempting a philosophical and scientific examination of religion and God's existence through performance, it questions God's existence by doubting the very means of communication through text, space, movement, music, light and video.

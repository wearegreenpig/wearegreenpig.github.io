Ipjeong-hada

About the work

A promenade performance through Ipjeong-dong in Euljiro, which is disappearing to redevelopment. Following a curator through the alleys, the audience meets objects placed here and there, performers turned into statues and exhibited sounds, and passing through performances and happenings in which several times are mixed, they "sense" a vanishing space. The performers become curators, moving statues and excavators of ruins, making a "living museum" that holds on to fading traces.

Performances

2022.3.3–4 / around Ipjeong-dong - produced by Geo, Geu, Gi, Green Pig and MiMi Project

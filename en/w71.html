Proud Mary

About the work

Mary came from Vietnam to Korea dreaming of becoming a K-pop idol. What awaits her is not a glittering stage but Club 'Proud' in Dongducheon. A story set in the camptown that grew around the U.S. military base in Dongducheon. Winner of the 2025 ARKO × Byeoksan Playwriting Award.

Performances

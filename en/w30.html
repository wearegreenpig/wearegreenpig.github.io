At the Pasqueflower Bar, Grandma Said She'd Give Us Melon Seeds

About the work

A story of children abused by their stepmother who dream of seeking asylum. It recalls a nation ruled by unjust power with evil intentions, and the children who, far from breathing under it, could only disappear. Part of the invited program "Sewol 2017" (2017.7.6–8.13) by the 6th members of Theater Lab Hyehwa-dong 1beonji, co-produced by Green Pig.

Performances

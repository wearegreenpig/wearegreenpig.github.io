Ill-fated: A Twentieth Birthday Wish

About the work

On Christmas, a matricide takes place in Choeun-dong. Siyeon, charged with killing her mother, will not speak. In her delusions she becomes Kate, a psychiatrist caring for a patient named Diana and sparring with her colleague Farren, but the tragedy of reality seeps into her head. In scenes where delusion and reality overlap, the work portrays a slice of an age in which a survivor of child abuse who committed parricide lives alongside those who looked away from the abuse or forgot it.

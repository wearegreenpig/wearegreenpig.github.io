Ankle

About the work

It begins from the origin myth of the Göktürks, the tale of a wolf that raised a child whose ankles had been cut off by feeding him meat. Dan, who lost his ankle when his right foot was pulled into a conveyor belt while working for a subcontractor at the Seohae thermal power plant, and Zaya, who came from Mongolia and works at a moving company. Through the love of two people who begin to care for each other's bodies that no one else cares about, playwright Kang Hoongu speaks of industrial accidents among young irregular workers and the problems of migrant workers, and asks about the "love" needed for a severed body to become a new body.

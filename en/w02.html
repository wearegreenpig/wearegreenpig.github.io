I Am Happy


2007, Jeongbo Theater, Daehangno

About the work

A riverbank at dawn: a father, a son and big suitcases. A child who left the forest and his family and crossed the river for a better life suffers unfair treatment and job loss in a strange land, and goes looking for the factory where Muhammad works with the address his father gave him. From the Koreans who left for the henequen plantations of Mexico a hundred years ago to today's migrant workers, a story that follows, sees off and welcomes the footsteps of those who leave.

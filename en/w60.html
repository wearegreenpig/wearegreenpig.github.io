In Search of the Lost Body

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. April. The body reacts sensitively to its surroundings, and the history of one's life piles up in the body. The piece examines the record the body accumulated over a year, between April 2023 and April 2024, and tries to wake a body stiffened by habit.

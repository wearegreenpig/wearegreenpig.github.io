Haemun of Jochiwon

About the work

In 2012, as Jochiwon-eup in Yeongi County, South Chungcheong, was being turned into Sejong Special Self-Governing City, land prices soared and the town was booming. Against this backdrop the play rewrites Shakespeare's Hamlet. With Koreanized characters like "Haemun" and "Opiri," thick Chungcheong dialect, ssireum wrestling, shamanic rites and a play within a play, it pokes comically and sharply at the wounds that development policy and fetishism have left on our society. Motifs of "two" such as doubling, twins, repetition and mirrors make up its form and structure. Winner of the 4th Byeoksan Playwriting Award, 2014.

Performances

2015.8.28–9.13 / National Theater Company, Baek Seonghui & Jang Minho Theater - 4th Byeoksan Playwriting Award winner

Yanbian Mother

About the work

Jeon Huibok, a former army general with dementia; his combative son Jeon Gangdon; a mother obsessed with getting her daughter into medical school; a son who calls himself progressive; and a daughter who wants to go to France. Into their luxury apartment comes Bok Gilsun, a housekeeper from Yanbian, whose goals are to find her daughter, out of touch in Korea, and to earn money for her injured son's surgery. Starting from the likeness between a family and the society around it, it depicts the Jeon household and the cities of the South heading toward ruin like two clocks.

Performances

2011.5.27–6.12 / Daehangno Arts Theater, Main Hall

Blooming Station: Traces

About the work

The work deals with social traumas of modern Korean history that happened in April. At a station where the family of a Sewol ferry survivor stays, victims of Jeju 4.3 in 1948, the April 19 Revolution of 1960 and the Sabuk uprising of April 1980 gather one by one. A quiet work of mourning that looks at people who could not catch the bus called history and were left behind like traces. Jung Yujin's first production as director.

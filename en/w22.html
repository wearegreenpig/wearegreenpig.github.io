Young Fucidin

About the work

The actors get angry at every single line throughout. A work that reads situations which can only make you angry before reading the emotions, capturing Korean society's problems of poverty, old age and housing through playwright Yoon Mihyun's distinctive eye.

Performances

2014.2.21–3.2 / Arko Arts Theater, Small Hall

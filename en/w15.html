Garden Killer

About the work

A family lives in a shoe-repair stall. Jinro's dad and his elder brother, who drink every day waiting for war to really break out; the brother's girlfriend, Agassi; a mother who left home to get double-eyelid surgery; and Grandma Gollum, who keeps the family alive by picking lettuce, squash and peppers from other people's gardens and flowerbeds to sell. In Gollum's mouth are the family's entire fortune: three gold teeth. Like grass that barely sprouts between the paving blocks of the city, the story of an organic family living not in a solid house but in the open. The first work of ARKO's "Spring Writer, Winter Stage 2012."

Performances

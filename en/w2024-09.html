Liar Game

About the work

History Dispute Project: a three-year project that rejects the victors' point of view and rewrites history from our own. Throughout 2024 Green Pig staged one performance a month, each starting from an event of the same month a year earlier. September. It began with the word "communist totalitarianism." Accident or incident? Victim or casualty? The word that wins the clash takes the power. Repeating collisions between statements, the piece casts doubt on the way history is made in a language someone else leads.
